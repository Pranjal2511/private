To my favourite person,

Three years. Somehow that sounds both so big and still not nearly enough time with you.

When I think about us, I don't only think about the big milestones. I think about the little things: the conversations that go on longer than planned, the random laughs, the comfort of knowing you're there, and all the moments that probably looked ordinary to everyone else but mean something to me.

Thank you for being part of my life, for the memories we've made, and for the person I keep getting to know. I know neither of us is perfect, and I don't expect every day to be perfect either. I just hope we keep trying to understand each other, keep being kind, and keep choosing this little team of ours.

I can't fit three years of feelings into one website or one letter. But I can tell you this: what we have matters to me, and I'm grateful for every chapter we've shared.

Happy three years, my love. Here's to more silly moments, honest talks, unexpected adventures, and a thousand small reasons to smile. And when life gets busy, I hope we remember to keep making room for the small things that brought us here.

If I could go back to the start, knowing everything I know now, I would still want to meet you, learn you, laugh with you, and make this story ours. There is so much I still want to experience with you. For now, I just want you to know that you are loved, appreciated, and chosen.

Happy 3rd anniversary, my love.

Yours, with all my heart ♥
